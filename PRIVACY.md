# Privacy Policy — Steady Life Notebook

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Steady Life Notebook ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, "든든 생활수첩" (@deundeun-lifenote).

## What the app accesses
The app requests a single Google permission: `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload`. It is used only to upload videos, set their thumbnails and schedule their publish time on the YouTube channel of the Google account that authorized it. The app does not read, list, change, or delete any other YouTube or Google data.

## What data is stored
- An OAuth token for the owner's own Google account, stored privately on the owner's computer.
- No personal data of any other person is collected.

## Sharing
The app does not sell, share, or disclose any user data to third parties. The token is never published.

## Retention and deletion
The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions , which invalidates the stored token.

## Google API Services User Data Policy
Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
