# Privacy Policy — Steady Life Notebook

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Steady Life Notebook ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, "든든생활노트" (@deundeun-lifenote).

## YouTube API Services
The app uses **YouTube API Services**. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
Information is also handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) ( http://www.google.com/policies/privacy ).

## What the app accesses
The app requests a single Google permission: `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload`. It is used only to:
- upload videos to the YouTube channel of the Google account that authorized it (`videos.insert`), including scheduling their publish time, and
- set the thumbnail of those videos (`thumbnails.set`).

The app does not read, list, change, or delete any other YouTube or Google data, and it does not access any other user's data.

## What data is stored
- An OAuth token for the owner's own Google account, stored only on the owner's computer.
- No personal data of any other person is collected, stored, or shared. The app does not use cookies or analytics.

## Sharing
The app does not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. The token is never published.

## Revoking access and deleting data
- Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (also https://myaccount.google.com/permissions ). Revoking access invalidates the stored token.
- The locally stored token is deleted by the owner when access is revoked, and in any case within 30 days of the app no longer being used.
- To request deletion or ask questions, contact the email below; requests are handled within 7 days.

## Google API Services User Data Policy
Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
