# Privacy Policy — Steady Life Notebook

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Steady Life Notebook ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, "든든생활노트" (@deundeun-lifenote).

## YouTube API Services
The app uses **YouTube API Services**. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
Information is also handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) ( http://www.google.com/policies/privacy ).

## What the app accesses
The app requests these Google permissions, only for the channel of the Google account that authorized it:
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload` — to upload the owner's videos (`videos.insert`, including scheduling their publish time) and set their thumbnails (`thumbnails.set`).
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly` — **read-only**, to read the owner's own channel statistics (views, average view percentage, subscribers gained, likes) so the owner can see which topics perform well.
- `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl` — to manage the owner's own videos on request of the owner: list them, edit titles/descriptions/publish schedule/thumbnails, organize them into the owner's playlists, and read comments on them. The app has no delete function.

The app does not access, change, or delete any other user's or channel's data. Statistics are stored only on the owner's computer as a local report.

## What data is stored
- OAuth tokens for the owner's own Google account and local copies of the owner's own channel statistics, stored only on the owner's computer.
- No personal data of any other person is collected, stored, or shared. The app does not use cookies or analytics.

## Sharing
The app does not sell, share, or disclose any data to third parties. The token is never published.

## Revoking access and deleting data
- Access can be revoked at any time via the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (also https://myaccount.google.com/permissions ). Revoking access invalidates the stored token.
- The locally stored token is deleted by the owner when access is revoked, and in any case within 30 days of the app no longer being used.
- To request deletion or ask questions, contact the email below; requests are handled within 7 days.

## Google API Services User Data Policy
Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
